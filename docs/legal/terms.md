## What Sonear is

Sonear is an independent desktop app that plays music from your Spotify account, or from YT Music. It is not made, sponsored or endorsed by Spotify, Google or YouTube.

For Spotify, it uses the Spotify Web API and Spotify Soloist, which you download from Spotify and use under Spotify's own terms. To use it you need a Spotify Premium account, your own Spotify developer app (a Client ID) and your own Soloist key. You must also follow the [Spotify Terms of Use](https://www.spotify.com/legal/end-user-agreement/).

For YT Music, it reads YT Music's web pages the way a browser does, not through an official API, and plays through two open-source tools, mpv and yt-dlp, which Sonear downloads from their GitHub releases when you ask it to. Each tool comes under its own license. You are responsible for following [YouTube's Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the laws where you live when you use this source.
